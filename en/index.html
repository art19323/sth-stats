What it shows: across the whole audience the US is 35.6% and Russia 19.3%, Belarus only 3.5% — its weight is diluted by Facebook and Spotify. In streaming Russia rises to 25.2% (Yandex and VK), while in social the US takes 42.1% and Belarus 5.6%. Your local audience lives in streaming and TikTok; the Western one lives in Facebook and Spotify.

Takeaways

  1. Spikes are tied to specific videos, not to the calendar. The official TikTok peak on October 1–3 is one video, “The Pit. Jumpstyle under metal” (75,547 views). The fan-page peak on October 13–16 is “set to hate - delirium” (902,140).
  2. YouTube reacts to a premiere with a 2–3 day lag. The video went live on 09/18, views topped out on 09/20–21, then a flat decline with no overnight tail.
  3. On Facebook the hook wins, not the band name. Best post of the month, “This is underground!”, pulled 769,964 views and 36,462 interactions versus 211,357 for the direct “The Pit is out now!”. Best retention belongs to Delirium from 10/05: 13.2 s of 16 (82%). Unclear hook plus short clip beats a straight announcement.
  4. The fan-page viral gave an echo in VK Music. On October 13–15 saves rose to 55/53/40 a day — people went looking for the track after TikTok, with no links anywhere.
  5. Conversion to a follow is 0.03–0.4%. 1.55M fan-page views produced +527 followers; 960K on Instagram, +3,106; 5.19M on Facebook, 18 link clicks. Meanwhile one Reels, “Super hot!”, collected 28,865 shares on October 15 — the content travels, the account does not.
  6. Telegram: reach falls after pauses. After 13 silent days the October 17 post got 169 views, the month's low, yet 45 reactions, the month's best ER. The audience stayed; the reach did not.
  7. YouTube: Shorts bring views, clips bring time. 134 Shorts are 47% of views, but 10 clips are 82% of watch time. There are no playlists at all, and playlists were exactly what gave the best retention (2:15).
Sources: TikTok Studio (both accounts, 4 tabs), Meta Business Suite (Instagram, Facebook), YouTube Studio (advanced mode: content, traffic sources, geography, cities, demographics), Spotify for Artists (tracks, playlists, audience, daily series), VK Music, Vauvision (distributor), BandLink, Telegram channel export (188 posts).
